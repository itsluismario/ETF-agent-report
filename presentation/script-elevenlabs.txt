===== CLIP 2 · 0:12 – 0:30 · The problem =====
Fidelity's Investly app helps people invest in ETFs. <break time="0.3s" /> But once the money is in, most clients stop paying attention. The fund page shows only names and weights. <break time="0.3s" /> Research shows that investors who understand why their investments moved stay more loyal, while those who trade more earn less.

===== CLIP 3 · 0:30 – 0:42 · Goal and scope =====
Our goal: keep ETF investors involved every month, without asking them to trade. <break time="0.3s" /> Each month they get a report on their own ETF: which companies moved it, and by how much.

===== CLIP 4 · 0:42 – 1:05 · The solution in one picture =====
Here's how it flows. <break time="0.3s" /> On day one, n8n downloads the full holdings list from Fidelity, and an AI agent matches every company to its stock ticker. <break time="0.3s" /> On days two to seven, n8n collects month-end prices. Postgres calculates the returns, an agent writes a summary, and the dashboard reads it live.

===== CLIP 5 · 1:05 – 1:15 · Built on Fidelity × n8n =====
Fidelity gives us the data and the question. <break time="0.2s" /> n8n is the engine: four workflows, two AI agents, a monthly schedule with no manual steps, and a chat that answers questions on the dashboard.

===== CLIP 7 · 2:06 – 2:36 · Innovation and impact =====
For the investor, it's a reason to come back every month, understand their money and ask questions in plain English, with no trades and nothing at risk. <break time="0.3s" /> For Fidelity and Investly, the research points the same way. Finimize found that seventy-four percent of its customers would invest more with more chances to learn. <break time="0.3s" /> And for one asset manager studied by S S and C, twenty-one percent higher retention meant thirty-three percent more revenue. <break time="0.3s" /> All of it runs automatically, on data Fidelity already has.
